AI Assistant for Odoo

Ask your Odoo anything, in plain language

A chat window inside Odoo that answers questions about your sales, purchases, invoices, stock, customers and CRM from your live data, with the access rights of the user who asks. Start with 20 free questions: install, go to Settings > AI Assistant and click Activate.

Why teams use it

  • Read-only: it never creates, changes or deletes anything.
  • Respects permissions: every read runs as the user who asked (access rights, record rules, companies).
  • Documents in one click: quotation, order, invoice and vendor bill PDFs, and links to open the records.
  • Accurate figures: totals, counts and rankings are computed by the database, not guessed by the AI.
  • Follows the conversation: "and the PDF of it?" works.
  • Replies in your language.

1. Activate in Settings

After installing, the chat stays hidden. Go to Settings > AI Assistant and click Activate: a free demo key is already filled in.

The Ask AI button and the AI Assistant app appear for users in the AI Assistant User group.

2. Ask questions

Security and privacy

  • Only read requests; executed as the logged-in user, never as administrator.
  • Passwords, API keys, system parameters, mail and payment credentials and attachments are never read.
  • Every question and data request is logged in Settings > AI Assistant Logs.
  • Sent to AAI Square: at activation, your company name, Odoo URL, version and server name; for each question, the question and the records needed to answer it. Answers are written by an AI model (OpenAI). Your database is never copied.

After the 20 free questions

Subscribe to Starter (1 database) or Pro (2 databases), both with unlimited questions. Paste your licence key in Settings > AI Assistant and click Activate.
